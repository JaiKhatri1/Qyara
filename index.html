Train It on Your Content

Qyara is a white-label AI chatbot platform. You train it once on your own content — website pages, uploaded files, manual Q&A, Notion, Google Drive or Calendly — and it answers customers by text or voice on your website, on WhatsApp, or through a shareable chat link. An admin console lets your team manage the knowledge base and watch every conversation.
ChatGPT is trained on the open internet and doesn't know your business. Qyara answers from a knowledge base you control, using retrieval over your own content, so replies reflect your products, policies and pricing. You can test answers privately in the Playground before anything goes live.
Open Knowledge Base → Website Upload and enter a URL. Qyara scrapes that page, or — with Crawl switched on — follows every in-scope link and ingests all of them automatically. You can also upload files, add Q&A by hand, or pull content from Notion, Google Drive and Calendly.
